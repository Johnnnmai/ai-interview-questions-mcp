# Privacy policy

ai-interview-questions-mcp runs entirely on your machine over stdio. The optional Streamable HTTP mode serves the same read-only tools and follows the same rules.

- It collects no personal data, analytics, or telemetry.
- It makes no network requests. All questions ship inside the package.
- It stores nothing. Your answers stay in your conversation with your AI client. The HTTP mode is stateless and keeps no sessions or request logs of its own; a hosting provider may keep standard access logs.

Links in tool results point to the public question bank site or the free IB Top 30 guide and carry `utm_source=mcp` so the site can count visits from this server. Opening a link is always your choice.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Johnnnmai/ai-interview-questions-mcp/issues or email press@valenx.org.
